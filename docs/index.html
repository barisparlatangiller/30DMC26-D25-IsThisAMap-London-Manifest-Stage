Is a stage a map?

Audience

Manifest, Intro & Hileli, live at OVO Arena Wembley, London. Six performers on 24 × 24 m of stage. Each one colours the floor she covers, and over 4½ minutes the song draws its own map.

0:26

    Moments

      How this was made

      Every 2 seconds the position of each performer was read from the concert video ( direct observations, the rest interpolated) and placed on an approximate stage plan: a 2.5 m platform at +3.3 m with stairs, an 8 m main stage, a 7.5 m runway and an 18 m front block. Screen positions are not surveyed coordinates, so every point is an estimate.

      Colour: each sample counts for 2 seconds of presence, strongest where she stands and gone 2.6 m away. Thin lines trace the path she has walked. Where several performers share the floor, the colour of whoever spent more time there leads, so the colours don't wash out to white. Clear sightings count fully, approximate ones 0.6 and silhouettes 0.4. Moments when she is out of frame are left out and shown as a dashed ring.
