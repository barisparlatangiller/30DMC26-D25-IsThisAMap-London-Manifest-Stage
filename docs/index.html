    Sync and method

    Positions were read by hand from the concert video every 2 seconds ( direct observations, the rest interpolated between them) and placed on an approximate plan of the stage: platform 2.5 m, main stage 8 m, runway 7.5 × 8 m, front block 18 × 8 m. Screen positions are not surveyed stage coordinates, so every point is an estimate.

    Heat: each sample counts for 2 seconds and fades to nothing about 1.8 m from the performer. Weights: clear 1.0, approximate 0.6, silhouette 0.4. "Not visible" moments (camera cuts, people out of frame) stay out of the heat map; they are drawn as hollow, dashed rings.
